## 2026-11-24: Walls, yields and wonder art painted over towns; towns are now the top of the land layer

- **What happened.** The designer saw Walls footings drawn across Ostia's houses, wheat icons on Capua's gatehouse, a natural wonder's painting over Mediolanum, and the "+25%" badge's text running into its own icon.
- **Causes.** (1) Every wall ring (finished in the terrain cache, footings in the construction overlay) was drawn whole on top of a town sprite that fills the same ground oval, so the far wall crossed the houses and the near wall sat on the front row. (2) The city's own tile drew its yield cluster at the hex's bottom centre, which is exactly where the town's oval lies (the art is dropped `CITY_DROP` below the hex centre and is 1.4 hex widths wide). (3) Natural-wonder paintings are drawn after the terrain cache, so their 2.5-radius span covered any town beside them. (4) Neighbours' medallions and yield clusters only avoided banners, not the town, whose art spills into their hexes. (5) The wonder badge sized its pill at 0.6 em a character; the real "+25%" is wider.
- **Fix.** Rings are cut at the oval's middle (`WALL_CUT`): back half, town at 0.78 scale, front half, all in the terrain cache (footings moved there too; the overlay no longer draws them). The wonder painting goes to a scratch canvas and the town's own sprites are cut out of it (`TerrainCache.stampTowns`). `townCover` is now the real footprint (`cityArtFootprint`) and is a cover for medallions, yield clusters (which slide sideways) and wonder badges (which get a higher slot before the bottom one). City tiles show no yield cluster. The badge measures its text.
- **Second pass (art review).** A single scale still left houses on the side ramparts and the star fort's bastions in the back half, because a ring's court is narrower above the cut line than at it. The town is now clipped to the ring's court (`courtMask`: flood fill of clear ground from the oval's centre, filled row by row, open upward so roofs still rise over the far wall), composed once per town and ring (`walledTown`).
- **Gotcha.** A ring's inner edge can't be measured from its alpha at the cut row (the Renaissance star fort is notched there); the gate test checks the scaled town ends inside the wall's outer edge less its thickness and skips the star fort.
