## 2026-11-23: Hard Rome waited out its own opening; the opening march fixes the timing (`rome_opening_march` on)

- **What happened.** Handed the designer's own Rome positions, Hard stalled: from his turn-10 position it had three melee and two of them near an unwalled rival city by turn 19, but declared on turn 24, by which time the city had Walls. In both of his Rome games he declared on turn 19 and struck on turn 20 with one or two Spearmen. Roman promotions (16 experience) come mostly from city hits (3 per hit, 10 for the hit that breaks the city), so a Rome that never assaults never promotes and, with veteran founding, never expands.
- **Change.** `rome_opening_march` defaults to 1 (`crates/ai/src/rome_opening.rs`): through turn 40 Hard Rome keeps the nearest reachable unwalled rival city as its opening target (the next softest if Walls appear), favours melee until it has three, declares once two are within three hexes, and keeps the force marching. It also fixes a pre-war path bug that made the city tile itself unreachable. Hard Rome only; it reads only its own view.
- **Evidence.** 200 paired Rome-seat games against five Hard seats: level-1 melee by turn 40 0.99 to 1.98 per game (+0.98 [+0.76, +1.21]), games with a capture by turn 50 86 to 144, VP share 8.0% to 9.5% (+1.45 [-0.24, +3.14]), own capital lost 59.0% to 58.0%. In the designer's Nov 22 seat from turn 1 (32 salts): median cities at turn 50 1 to 4 and at turn 100 2.5 to 5 (his: 6 and 11), level-1 melee by turn 40 0 to 3. All-Hard table, 2,000 games paired by seed: Rome 2.8% to 5.2% (paired +2.4 points [+0.8, +3.9]), every other faction inside its interval, best to worst 8.6x to 4.8x. Duel, 400 seeds (first look): B-seat VP share +0.17 [+0.02, +0.31], Rome seats +1.84 [+0.25, +3.43]. Scripted rusher gate (220 seeds): a Hard city taken by turn 60 40.9% to 37.7%; seats eliminated by the rusher 0.050 to 0.068 (n.s.). Recorded-attack cells unchanged.
- **Not solved.** Cities by turn 100 on fresh maps barely move (median 2 to 3). Rome still loses its capital in most six-seat games, late (after turn 100), because it stays small. An experimental gene that lets a level-0 Roman melee unit hit a standing city without a capture forecast added VP share on top (+2.28 [+0.37, +4.19] vs off) but no further promotions; it is not part of this change.
