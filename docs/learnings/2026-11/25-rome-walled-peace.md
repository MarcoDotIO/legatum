## 2026-11-25: Hard Rome held a lost war open for 25 turns; Peace frees the captured city (`rome_walled_peace` on)

- **What happened.** After taking the first city of its opening victim, Hard Rome almost never took the victim's last city: on two of the designer's maps the victim survived in 29 of 32 and 24 of 32 games. The victim walls that city on a routine timetable (from turn 15 or turns 24 to 29), not in reaction to the loss. The opening refused every Peace offer until turn 50, so the captured city stayed frozen by the occupation rule (no production) for a median 22 to 25 turns, with one unit, often Rome's only veteran, locked inside.
- **What did not work.** Marching on to the last city, an earlier or later second-war window, an early scout and keeping units in sight of the last city each failed the city race. The defender drops Walls for units only when it is at war and sees enough Roman strength to count a deficit at its door, and Hard Rome could not spare that strength from its first siege.
- **Change.** `rome_walled_peace` defaults to 1: once every remaining known city of the opening victim has observed completed Walls and a captured victim city is still locked, Hard Rome offers and accepts Peace instead of holding the war to turn 50, and its second war may start before turn 51 in that case. The defender still decides normally. Hard Rome only; it reads only its own view.
- **Evidence.** City race from the designer's three Rome starts, 32 paired runs each, median cities at turn 100: 7 to 7, 3 to 4.5 and 4 to 5 (paired better/tie/worse 4/28/0, 11/15/6, 8/21/3); frozen city-turns by turn 50 fell from 24 to 15.5 on one map. Cities lost and own capital lost unchanged in that race. In 200 paired Rome-seat games Rome lost its original capital by game end in 113 games against 126 (discordant 1 against 14) and cities at turn 50 rose +0.14 [+0.06, +0.22]; wins and VP share unchanged. All-Hard table, 2,000 games paired by seed: Rome 7.2% to 7.8% (paired +0.5 [-0.3, +1.4]), every faction inside its interval, best to worst 3.3x to 3.2x. Duel 400 seeds neutral; rusher gate and recorded-attack cells unchanged.
