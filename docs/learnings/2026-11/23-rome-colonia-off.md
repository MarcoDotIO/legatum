## 2026-11-23: Reserving founders for the Colonia Victrix cost Hard Rome its cities (`rome_colonia` now off)

- **What happened.** Hard Rome's win share in all-Hard games fell from 2.9% (rules 51) to 1.2% (rules 55, 2,000 games). A paired run on 400 Rome-seat games per arm (same seeds) put the drop in the rules 53 to 54 step: 10 wins before, 2 after, with fewer cities at turn 50 in 30 seats against 1 with more.
- **The cause is the gene `rome_colonia`.** On rules 55, turning only that gene off takes Rome from 3 wins in 400 to 10 and VP share from 6.10 to 7.22, back to the rules-53 level (9 seeds became wins, 2 stopped being wins; exact p 0.065, so treat the size as approximate). `goal_city_capture` off and `treasury_build_guard` off change nothing for Rome.
- **Why.** The Colonia was completed in 0 of 400 games in every arm: no Hard Rome seat reached level 3, and only 20 of 400 had a level-2 melee unit while the goal was open. The gene keeps the would-be founder out of ordinary founding and out of captures that would lock it, for a goal the AI never reaches, so it founds and captures less.
- **Checked for harm (rules 55, gene off for every seat).** All-Hard table, 2,000 games, paired by seed: Rome 1.2% to 2.7% [1.9, 3.8], paired +1.5 points [+0.4, +2.5]; every other faction stays inside its interval; best to worst ratio 20.9x to 9.4x. Scripted rusher gate, 220 seeds: a Hard city taken by turn 60 in 41.4% both ways. Recorded-attack cells unchanged.
- **Change.** `rome_colonia` defaults to 0. The code and its tests stay; the goal itself (rules 54) is unchanged for players. Turn it back on only together with a plan that gets units to level 3. With the gene off Hard Rome also gives no extra guard to a Colonia it founds by chance (`goal_cities.rs::holds_goal_city`); that is the measured arm and is left as it is.
- **Wider lesson.** A goal-pursuit gene needs a reach check before it is allowed to reserve units: measure how often the AI can meet the goal's precondition, not only how it behaves once it does. Hard Rome's real gap is earlier: it keeps 43% of its army within 2 hexes of home at turn 50 and fights half as many battles as a strong human opening.
