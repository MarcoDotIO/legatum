## 2026-11-24: Hard Rome stopped after its first victim; a second war adds cities (`rome_second_war` on)

- **What happened.** Handed the designer's own Rome positions at turns 40 and 60, Hard Rome almost never started another war by turn 80 (2 of 32 and 0 of 32 runs on one map), although it had four or more fit melee units. The designer opened a second and third war around turns 46 to 59 and kept two or three going.
- **Change.** `rome_second_war` defaults to 1 (`crates/ai/src/rome_second_war.rs`): between turns 51 and 100, once the opening victim has no reachable open city and Rome has four fit melee, it declares on the softest reachable neighbour, holds that war while an open city remains and keeps hitting it. Hard Rome only; it reads only its own view.
- **Evidence.** From the designer's turn-60 position on one map, median cities at turn 100 went from 9 to 14, better in all 32 paired runs. In 200 paired Rome-seat games: cities at turn 100 +0.27 [+0.08, +0.45], VP share unchanged, own capital lost by game end 26 against 19 discordant games (exact p 0.37). All-Hard table, 2,000 games paired by seed: Rome 6.4% to 7.2% (paired +0.8 [-0.7, +2.4]); Egypt, the strongest faction, fell 26.5% to 23.6% (paired -2.8 [-4.9, -0.7]) and Byzantium rose 1.6; best to worst 4.1x to 3.3x. Duel 400 seeds neutral; rusher gate and recorded-attack cells unchanged.
- **Not solved.** From turn 1 the designer's maps still end far below his city counts. A larger army before the second war, a chain of third wars, and late Walls in the capital were each tried and dropped.
